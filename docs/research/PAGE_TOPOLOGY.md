# LUMIÈRE Prime Hills page topology

The root route serves the rendered source HTML directly, preserving its section order and main-window scroll behavior:

1. Overlay menu, project mark, hotline, language selector, and lead CTA.
2. Full-viewport hero with architectural image, timed copy, and floating controls.
3. Project introduction and render.
4. Project facts / overview infographic with interactive tower affordances.
5. Experience values and editorial image sequence.
6. Body / mind / spirit nature story with layered media and numbered content.
7. Regional connectivity map, legend, and nearby facilities.
8. International development partners.
9. Amenity gallery with floor/category choices and 12 official renders.
10. Masterise Homes brand story.
11. News cards.
12. Contact details, registration form, legal/privacy information, and footer.

Original HTML, CSS, Next.js chunks, and locally mirrored media are served from `public/`. A small image route redirects the source Next image optimizer URLs to their downloaded local files.
